04 / NOTES FROM THE BENCH

Good to know.

Important information before the collection opens for minting.

What is CraftMeetCode?+

A collection of 10,000 craftsman-bot pixel-art collectibles. Each piece is generated by the same deterministic engine used for the previews on this site.

Is minting available yet?+

Not yet. The contract has not been deployed, and all transaction buttons are disabled. Do not send funds to an unannounced contract address.

Is the “free mint” really free?+

The free phase has no artwork price, but the planned contract charges a 0.0001 BNB service fee. Network gas will be charged separately if minting opens.

Which network is planned?+

BNB Chain. This site does not claim that a contract is already deployed. The address, final network, and metadata must be verified after deployment.

How can I verify a token and its metadata?+

Once the contract and metadata are announced, verify the address and transactions using the official blockchain explorer. Previews on this site are not proof of on-chain ownership.
